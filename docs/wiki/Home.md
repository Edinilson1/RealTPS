# RealTPS wiki

A guide for players: what each material can do, which one to take on which mission, and how to read the shield
window.

> The examples in this wiki come from the mod's numerical model of stock Kerbin entries
> (`docs/sources/MISSION_TEST_MATRIX.md`) and from my own test flights. They show how the materials compare, but
> your results will depend on your craft, your entry and your planet pack.

## Quick start

1. Install RealTPS and what it needs (ModuleManager, B9PartSwitch). See the README.
2. In the VAB or SPH, right-click a heat shield (or a Making History pod) and pick a **Thermal protection**. The
   tooltip of each option shows its limits, thickness, conductivity and mass.
3. You start with **cork-phenolic**. Better materials unlock in the tech tree; in career mode you buy the unlock in
   the R&D building, just like a part.
4. In flight, the part menu shows the material, how much is left and the integrity. **Shield diagnostics** in the
   part menu, or the toolbar button, opens the shield window.
